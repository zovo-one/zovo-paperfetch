The copy behind the paywall

The paper is right there. Find the free copy.

Authors and universities post free, legal full texts of many papers. Finding one means copying the title into open indexes by hand, and most readers give up at the paywall. PaperFetch does that checking for you.

Built for students, researchers, and anyone who reads past the abstract.

One click

Get the legal copy in one click.

PaperFetch reads the DOI the publisher already embeds in the page for indexing, then asks four open indexes in order. The first legally-posted copy opens in a new tab. If none exists, it says so.

Your library writes itself

Every lookup becomes a citation you keep on this device.

Each check lands in a local library on this device with the DOI, title, authors, journal, year, and where the free copy lived. Export it all as BibTeX whenever you like.

A library row is one DOI and its details, written by the extension, never typed by you. Try the shape of it here.
